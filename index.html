Omertá is the group Jay Alvarrez and Alex Smith started for founders, athletes and creatives who want more from life than the usual path. Members surf, jump, build companies and travel together.

Questions.

Who is it for?

Adventure, travel, sports and lifestyle creators who want to turn what they film into income. Beginners and early creators are welcome.

Do I need to quit my job?

The Lab is built to run alongside a job or studies.

Do I need a big audience to join?

The UGC path gets you paid by brands for content they use in their own ads, whatever the size of your following.

What gear do I need?

A phone or an action camera is enough to start. Students also get gear perks through Jay's camera partners.

Why is it by application?

Groups are small so every student gets real feedback from a coach. A short call makes sure the Lab is the right fit.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is shared on your application call.

Will I make money?

We promise reachable steps. The Lab gives you the craft, the system and the pitch, and results depend on the work you put in.
