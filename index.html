FAQ

Questions,answered.

Do I need to quit my job?

No. The Lab is built to run alongside a job or studies. Nobody has to quit to begin.

Do I need a big audience to join?

No. The UGC path gets you paid by brands for content they use in their own ads, with no following required.

What gear do I need?

A phone or an action camera is enough to start. Students also get gear perks through Jay's camera partners.

Why is it by application?

Groups are small so every student gets real feedback from a coach. A short call makes sure the Lab is the right fit. Not ready yet? On the call we offer the self-paced version: the Concept Engine top plan at $97 a month, with the tool, the Method and the Edit Kit.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is shared on your application call. The free masterclass and the Edit Kit are the easiest ways to start.

Will I make money?

We promise reachable steps, not income. The Lab gives you the craft, the system and the pitch; results depend on the work you put in.
